SURYAMIT

Your rooftop. Your solar future. Your smartest decision.

An AI-assisted marketplace where households get comparable rooftop-solar quotes from vendors, with transparent, code-calculated numbers.

Request→Quote→Compare→Accept
  • Introduce the team and the one-line idea: a marketplace that connects households and solar vendors.
  • Say the four verbs once: request, quote, compare, accept. Everything we built serves that flow.
  • Time: 0:30. Move on quickly to the problem.

The problem

Going solar today is slow, manual and confusing

1One by one

Households phone vendors individually and wait for separate site visits.

2Mixed formats

Quotes arrive as handwritten sheets, PDFs and phone photos. No two look alike.

3Is it fair?

Hard to judge whether a price, panel or warranty is good value.

4Subsidy maze

Subsidy and payback are hard to understand, so people hesitate or overpay.

Vendors have the mirror problem: they cannot easily find households that are ready to buy.
  • Walk the four pain points left to right. Keep each to one sentence.
  • Land the last line: both sides lose time. A shared, structured workflow fixes both.
  • Time: 1:00.

The solution

One workflow for households and vendors

🏠 Household

  • Publishes one request: map pin, roof photo, usage, budget
  • Sees estimates for subsidy and payback
  • Compares quotes side by side and accepts one

🔧 Vendor

  • Discovers open requests nearby on a list and map
  • Sends one structured quote in a common format
  • Tracks results in “My quotes”

⚙️ Platform

  • Calculates every number in tested code
  • Scores quotes with transparent weights
  • AI assists and explains, but never decides

Feature 5 · AI assistants

AI assists. It never decides.

📷 Roof photo

Describes visible obstructions such as water tanks and shade, with confidence in words and limits. Always reminds: a site survey is still needed. Consent first.

🎙️ Voice entry

Speak units and budget. Our own number parser goes first (“dedh lakh”), AI only if needed. You confirm before anything is saved.

📄 Quote extraction

Paste or upload a quotation and the form pre-fills. Text inside a document is never obeyed: injected instructions cannot change a score.

💬 Advisor

Explains the results in plain words. Its text is checked against the computed numbers. Anything that does not match is thrown away.

If AI is slow or unavailable, every flow still works: manual entry, template summaries, no blocked screens.
Try itPhoto step: tick consent, Analyze roof photoUsage step: Start voice input (Chrome)Vendor quote page: Extract from document
  • Four assistants, one rule: AI proposes, code and the user decide.
  • Honest point if the live AI is not connected on the demo machine: say that the screens show "analysis unavailable, you can continue", which is exactly the designed fallback. All four are tested with a simulated AI provider.
  • Prompt-injection defense is a hackathon bonus point: untrusted text is labelled as data, output is validated against a strict schema, scores come only from code.
  • Time: 1:30.
